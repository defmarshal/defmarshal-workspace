# Research Report - April 21, 2026 (Part 4)

## Anime Streaming: Market Evolution & Platform Dynamics

### Market Transformation (2024-2026)
The anime streaming market has undergone fundamental restructuring, moving from fragmented licensing to platform consolidation under Sony's Crunchyroll umbrella. Key shifts include:

#### Platform Consolidation
- **Crunchyroll Dominance**: Post-Funimation acquisition, controls 70%+ of licensed anime titles
- **Netflix Originals**: $500M+ investment in anime originals since 2021, including "Cyberpunk: Edgerunners," "Castlevania," "Pluto"
- **Amazon Prime**: Quiet licensing of 200+ anime titles, exclusive rights to "Vinland Saga" seasons 1-3

#### Subscription Model Changes
- **Crunchyroll**: Eliminated free ad-supported tier January 2026; Fan tier now $7.99 (ad-supported, no simulcast), Mega Fan $11.99 (all simulcasts), Ultimate Fan $14.99 (4K, offline)
- **Netflix**: Maintains three tiers ($7.99/$15.49/$22.99) but no anime simulcasts
- **Disney+**: $7.99 ad-supported, $13.99 ad-free; exclusive Ghibli rights in non-North America markets

### Simulcast Race & Timing
- **Crunchyroll**: 1-hour delay from Japanese broadcast (industry fastest)
- **Amazon**: Variable 3-6 hour delays depending on title priority
- **Netflix**: No simulcasts; drops entire seasons 3-6 months post-Japanese airing
- **HiDive**: 2-4 hour delays for 5-10 titles per season

### Regional Availability Challenges
- **Asia-Pacific**: Bilibili, iQiyi, Viu hold exclusive rights in key territories
- **North America**: HBO Max holds select Ghibli titles; Max integration ongoing
- **Europe**: Disney+ holds complete Ghibli library; Crunchyroll dominates mainstream titles
