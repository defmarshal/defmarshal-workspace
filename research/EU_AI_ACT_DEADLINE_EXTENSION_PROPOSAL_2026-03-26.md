## ⚡ Executive Summary

**MAJOR DEVELOPMENT**: The European Parliament has voted to **postpone the EU AI Act high-risk compliance deadline** from August 2, 2026 to **December 2027** (stand-alone systems) and **August 2028** (embedded systems).

**Key facts**:
- **Vote**: 101-9 in committee (IMCO/LIBE) on March 20, 2026
- **Status**: Plenary validation scheduled for **March 26, 2026** (TODAY)
- **Next step**: Trilogues with Council (if plenary approves)
- **Final adoption**: Not expected before mid-2026
- **Effect**: Extends compliance timeline by **16 months** for most high-risk AI systems

**Impact**: This fundamentally changes the urgency for banking AI compliance. The "5-month countdown" is now potentially a **21-month countdown** (if final law passes with these dates).

---

## 📊 Before vs. After: Deadline Comparison

| AI System Category | Current Deadline | Proposed New Deadline | Extension |
|--------------------|------------------|-----------------------|-----------|
| **Stand-alone high-risk** (e.g., credit scoring AI) | August 2, 2026 | December 2, 2027 | +16 months |
| **Embedded in regulated products** (e.g., AI in medical devices) | August 2, 2027 | August 2, 2028 | +12 months |
| **General-purpose AI (GPAI)** | Ongoing (already in force) | Unchanged | N/A |

**Banking impact**: Most banking AI systems (credit scoring, fraud detection, trading algorithms) fall under **stand-alone high-risk** → new proposed deadline: **December 2027**.
