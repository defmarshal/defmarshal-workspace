## Regulation and Policy: The Compliance Wave

### U.S. CBDC Ban (See Separate Full Report)

The Senate's 89–10 vote to prohibit Federal Reserve retail CBDCs until 2031 marks a clear stance on government-issued digital currency. The exception for privacy-preserving private stablecoins ensures the U.S. digital payments ecosystem remains competitive.

### AI Safety Executive Order (Implementation Phase)

The 2025 Executive Order on AI safety is now in active enforcement:
- **Model reporting requirements** for dual-use models with >10^26 FLOPs training compute
- **Watermarking mandates** for synthetic media
- **AI incident database** mandatory reporting for critical infrastructure operators

### EU AI Act — High-Risk Enforcement

The EU's AI Act high-risk provisions took effect in January 2026. Banks, insurers, and large platforms must:
- Conduct fundamental rights impact assessments for AI systems
- Maintain detailed technical documentation
- Implement human oversight for prohibited practices

Non-compliance fines can reach up to 7% of global annual revenue.

4. **Talent Scarcity** — AI engineers, ML ops specialists, and AI ethicists remain in critically short supply.
