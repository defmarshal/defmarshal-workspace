## 1. Background: The "RAMmageddon" Memory Shortage

### 1.1. What Is "RAMmageddon"?
The term "RAMmageddon" refers to the **protracted shortage of DRAM and NAND flash memory** that began in early 2024 and has persisted through 2025–2026 [2]. Key characteristics:

- **Price volatility**: DRAM spot prices surged 80% in 2024 and remain elevated
- **Supply constraints**: Limited wafer output at sub-10nm nodes (1α nm, 1β nm)
- **Demand surge**: AI servers, high-performance computing, and next-gen consoles require unprecedented memory bandwidth and capacity
- **Lead time extensions**: Orders stretch to 6–12 months for high-density modules

The shortage has impacted:
- **PC and laptop manufacturers** (reduced shipments, higher costs)
- **AI hardware vendors** (NVIDIA, AMD) constrained by memory availability
- **Automotive sector** (芯片短缺导致 production delays)
- **Consumer electronics** (console shortages, memory premium upgrades)

### 1.2. Root Causes
Multiple converging factors created the shortage:
1. **Underinvestment in capacity**: Memory manufacturers cut capex during 2022–2023 low cycle
2. **Technical challenges**: EUV lithography adoption delays at 1α nm and below
3. **Geopolitical disruptions**: Taiwan/China tensions raised supply chain risks
4. **AI demand spike**: HBM3E and GDDR7 demand exceeded forecasts by 200%+ [3]

Samsung and SK hynix, controlling ~70% of DRAM capacity, have been cautious about rapid expansion to avoid repeating the 2023 glut.
