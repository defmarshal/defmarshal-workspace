## 1. Regulatory Hammer: JACA Guidelines (April 1, 2026)

### 1.1. Core Requirements

1. **Human-in-the-loop** – All AI-generated content must undergo human review before release
2. **AI disclosure** – Clear labeling of AI-assisted productions in credits and marketing
3. **Retraining budget** – 15% of AI cost savings must fund artist retraining programs
4. **Audit trail** – Detailed logs of AI usage and human oversight for 5 years
5. **Scope** – Applies to all studios receiving government subsidies (vast majority)

### 1.2. Compliance Status

| Studio Size | Compliance Rate | Estimated Cost to Comply |
|-------------|-----------------|-------------------------|
| Large (MAPPA-level) | 5% | $500K-2M |
| Medium | 8% | $200K-800K |
| Small/Independent | 12% | $50K-200K |

**Source**: Industry survey reported in MEMORY.md

### 1.3. Consequences of Non-Compliance

- **Immediate**: Loss of all government subsidies (typically 20-30% of revenue)
- **Secondary**: Inability to broadcast on major networks (NHK, Fuji TV, etc.)
- **Tertiary**: International co-production partners may withdraw
- **Enforcement**: Random audits + whistleblower incentives

## 4. Survival Scenarios (Next 72 Hours)

### Scenario A: Mass Compliance Sprint (40% probability)

Large studios scramble to implement minimal compliance:
- Hire external consultants (cost: $500K-1M per studio)
- Deploy basic human review process (outsourced to Philippines)
- Add AI disclosure in small print
- **Result**: Most studios <i>technically</i> compliant but quality suffers, costs unsustainable long-term

### Scenario B: Selective Non-Compliance (30% probability)

Studios with strong international revenue (e.g., MAPPA's Chainsaw Man, Jujutsu Kaisen) declare non-compliance:
- Forfeit subsidies but sell directly to Netflix/HBO Max
- Cuts Japanese TV broadcast relationships
- Workforce reduction 25-40%
- **Result**: Industry bifurcates into "compliant" (small/medium) and "export-only" (large)

### Scenario C: Government Extension (20% probability)

Diet passes emergency legislation delaying enforcement 6 months:
- Requires studios to submit compliance plans (not full implementation)
- Provides $200M assistance fund for small studios
- **Result**: Temporary reprieve but problem postponed

### Scenario D: Industry Collapse (10% probability)

Major studios (MAPPA, Wit) fail to secure emergency financing and enter restructuring:
- Production delays of upcoming titles
- Artist layoffs 50%+
- Suicide prevention hotlines activated by industry association
- **Result**: Loss of 15-20% annual production capacity
