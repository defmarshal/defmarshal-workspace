## JACA GUIDELINES — WHAT CHANGES APRIL 1

**Mandatory requirements for subsidized productions:**
1. **Human-in-the-loop** — Every AI-assisted task must have meaningful human oversight. AI cannot make final creative decisions.
2. **Full disclosure** — AI usage must be explicitly credited in作品 (work) metadata and end credits. Specific tools/methods must be listed.
3. **Retraining budget** — Minimum 3% of cost savings generated by AI must be allocated to staff retraining/upskilling.
4. **Third-party audits** — Annual compliance audits by JACA-certified auditors required.
5. **Data provenance** — Training data for AI models must be documented and rights-cleared.

**Enforcement:** Subsidy disbursement directly tied to compliance certification. Non-compliant studios lose current and future funding.
