# AIDABench: AI Data Analytics Benchmark

As AI-powered document understanding and processing tools move from research labs into real-world business workflows, a critical question emerges: How do we know these systems actually work? Garbage in, gospel out doesn't cut it when enterprises rely on AI to extract insights from contracts, reports, and spreadsheets. That's where **AIDABench** comes in — a much‑needed benchmark that puts AI data analytics tools through their paces with rigor and relevance.

## Why AIDABench Matters

Organizations are deploying AI assistants to digest mountains of documents, but evaluating their performance has been haphazard. Existing benchmarks often focus on narrow NLP tasks or synthetic questions that don't capture the complexity of real data analysis. AIDABench fills this gap by offering a standardized suite of document understanding tasks that mirror actual business use cases — from extracting tabular data and answering multi‑hop questions to reasoning across multiple files.

## What Makes It Different

- **Real‑world document diversity**: PDFs, spreadsheets, presentations, and mixed‑format reports, not just clean text.
- **Task variety**: Includes extraction, summarization, numerical reasoning, and cross‑document QA.
- **Fine‑grained metrics**: Goes beyond accuracy to measure speed, resource usage, and robustness to format noise.
- **Open and extensible**: Researchers and practitioners can contribute new tasks and domains.

## Key Evaluation Dimensions

AIDABench assesses AI systems across several axes:

- **Document understanding fidelity** — Can the model correctly parse tables, headers, and hierarchical structures?
- **Reasoning depth** — Does the AI perform genuine logical inference, or just pattern matching?
- **Multi‑modal integration** — How well does it handle documents that combine text, numbers, and visual elements?
- **Scalability** — Performance on lengthy reports and large document collections.
- **Explainability** — Whether the system can cite source locations and show its work.

## The Road Ahead

AIDABench isn't just another academic dataset — it's a call to raise the bar for AI in enterprise analytics. By providing a common ground for comparison, it helps buyers make informed choices and pushes developers toward tools that truly understand data, not just mimic it. As AI adoption accelerates, benchmarks like this will become the trusted yardsticks that separate hype from real productivity gains.

In the end, AIDABench reminds us that intelligent systems need intelligent evaluation. The future of AI‑driven analytics depends on it.
