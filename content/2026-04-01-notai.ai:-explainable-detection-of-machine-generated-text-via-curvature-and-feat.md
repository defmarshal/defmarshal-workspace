# NOTAI.AI: Explainable Detection of Machine-Generated Text via Curvature and Feature Attribution

Ever wondered if that essay, product review, or social media post was written by a human or an AI? As language models get better at mimicking human writing, spotting machine-generated text has become a cat-and-mouse game. Enter **NOTAI.AI**—a new explainable framework that doesn't just say "this is AI-generated," but shows *why* and *how* it knows. By combining curvature-based signals with feature attribution, NOTAI.AI offers a transparent, interpretable solution to one of AI's trickiest challenges.

---

## 🔍 The Problem with Black-Box Detectors

Most existing AI text detectors are black boxes. They take in text and spit out a probability score, but offer no insight into *what* gave it away. Was it the sentence structure? Word choice? Something about the topic distribution? This lack of explainability is problematic for high-stakes applications like academic integrity, journalism, and legal contexts where you need to justify a decision. Additionally, detectors often fail to generalize across models or degrade when faced with paraphrasing attacks.

---

## 🌊 What Makes NOTAI.AI Different?

NOTAI.AI builds on Fast-DetectGPT's approach of comparing log probabilities between models, but adds two powerful new dimensions:

### 1. Curvature-Based Signals
The framework analyzes the **curvature of the probability landscape** around the text. Human writing tends to explore diverse semantic regions, while AI-generated text often follows paths of least resistance—leading to characteristic curvature patterns. By measuring how quickly probabilities change across nearby alternatives, NOTAI.AI captures subtle distributional differences that raw scores miss.

### 2. Feature Attribution for Transparency
Using techniques inspired by SHAP and Integrated Gradients, NOTAI.AI attributes detection decisions to specific **words, phrases, or syntactic structures**. You get a heatmap showing which parts of the text most strongly indicate machine generation. This isn't just a score—it's a forensic report.

---

## 🧪 How It Works (Without the Jargon)

At a high level, NOTAI.AI runs three steps:

1. **Log Probability Comparison**: Pass the suspect text through multiple language models (including reference models) to get token-level log probabilities.
2. **Curvature Estimation**: Sample slight variations of the text (synonym swaps, paraphrases) and measure how much the log probabilities change. AI text often shows *low curvature*—small changes lead to predictable probability shifts—while human text is more chaotic.
3. **Aggregation & Attribution**: Combine curvature signals with traditional divergence metrics, then back-propagate attribution scores to identify the most AI-like tokens.

The result? A detector that's both **more accurate** and **explainable**.

---

## 📈 Impressive Results from the Paper

Benchmark tests on several datasets (including cross-model generalization) show:

- **Detection accuracy** improvements of 5–12% over Fast-DetectGPT and OpenAI's classifier.
- **Robustness to paraphrasing**: NOTAI.AI maintains >85% accuracy even when text is paraphrased by GPT-4 itself.
- **Explainability quality** rated higher by human evaluators—the attribution maps actually highlight words that humans recognize as "AI-ish" (e.g., overly normative phrases, generic transitions).
- **Efficient**: adds only ~20% overhead compared to baseline detectors.

## ✨ Why Explainability Matters Here

Imagine flagging a student's essay as AI-generated. Without an explanation, it's just an accusation. With NOTAI.AI's attribution, you can say: *"The detector flagged this because of repetitive transitional phrases and unusually uniform sentence lengths."* That's actionable feedback—the student can learn and improve. In content moderation, it helps moderators understand *why* a post was labeled synthetic, reducing appeals. In journalism, it protects against false positives that could damage reputations.

---

## 🚀 The Bigger Picture: Toward Trustworthy AI Discourse

NOTAI.AI isn't just another detector—it's a step toward **accountable AI-generated content**. As AI-written text floods the internet, we need tools that can keep up not just in accuracy but in transparency. By opening the black box, NOTAI.AI sets a new standard: detection should be *auditable*, *justifiable*, and *educational*.
