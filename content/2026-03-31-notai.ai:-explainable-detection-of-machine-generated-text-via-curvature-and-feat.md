# NOTAI.AI: Explainable Detection of Machine-Generated Text via Curvature and Feature Attribution

AI-generated text is everywhere—from customer service chats to news articles, from academic papers to social media posts. While helpful, it also raises concerns about authenticity, plagiarism, and misinformation. Detecting machine‑written content has become a cat‑and‑mouse game; as language models improve, so do their ability to mimic human quirks. But detection tools have remained black boxes: they flag something as “likely AI” without explaining *why*. Enter **NOTAI.AI**, an open‑source framework that not only spots machine‑generated text but also tells you *which parts* look artificial and *what patterns* triggered the alarm. It’s like giving every AI‑generated sentence a transparency label.

## Beyond Fast‑DetectGPT: Adding Curvature Signals

NOTAI.AI builds on the popular Fast‑DetectGPT, which compares a text’s likelihood under different language models to spot anomalies. The new twist? **Curvature‑based signals**. In simple terms, curvature measures how quickly the model’s confidence changes across nearby tokens. Human‑written text tends to have smoother, more gradual probability shifts, while machine‑generated text often exhibits sharper jumps—a telltale sign of sampling from a distribution that isn’t perfectly calibrated. By capturing this geometric property, NOTAI.AI gains a richer signal than raw log‑probability differences alone.

## Feature Attribution: Pinpointing the Suspicious Bits

A major innovation is **integrated feature attribution**. Instead of a single “AI or not” verdict, NOTAI.AI highlights specific words, phrases, or sentences that look machine‑like. It uses techniques inspired by SHAP/LIME to attribute detection decisions back to input tokens. For example, if an essay claims “the utilization of resources leads to synergistic outcomes,” the extension might underline “utilization” and “synergistic” as high‑frequency LLM favorites. This explainability helps users understand the reasoning and builds trust in the tool.

## A Practical Browser Extension for Everyday Use

NOTAI.AI isn’t just a research codebase—it’s built as a **browser extension**. You install it, and as you read any webpage, the extension runs in the background:  
- It fetches the visible text.  
- Computes curvature and attribution scores locally (privacy‑preserving).  
- Overlays colored highlights: red for likely AI‑generated spans, blue for uncertain, none for human‑like.  
- Clicking a highlight shows a tooltip with the contributing features (“low perplexity variation”, “high token overlap with GPT‑4”).  

The extension also provides an overall trust score for the page, updated in real time as you scroll.

## Performance: More Accurate and More Transparent

Benchmarks on several datasets (student essays, news articles, Reddit posts) show that NOTAI.AI outperforms Fast‑DetectGPT in detection accuracy, especially on texts from newer models like GPT‑4o and Claude 3.5. More importantly, user studies indicate that **explanations increase acceptance** of the detector’s verdicts. When people see *why* a sentence was flagged, they’re less likely to dismiss the tool as biased or broken—a crucial factor for adoption in education and journalism.

---

As AI‑generated content floods the internet, we need detectors that are both powerful and understandable. NOTAI.AI demonstrates that explainability isn’t a luxury—it’s a necessity for building trust and enabling informed judgment. By combining curvature signals with feature attribution, it offers a window into the model’s mind, helping us navigate an increasingly synthetic information landscape. The future of content authentication isn’t just about catching AI; it’s about doing so transparently, responsibly, and with respect for human agency. NOTAI.AI takes us a step closer to that future.
